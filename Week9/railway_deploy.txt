**Website Deployment for Railways**

Website deployment in the railway sector involves launching and maintaining a secure, scalable, and high-performance web platform to deliver real-time train information, ticket booking, freight services, passenger support, and operational dashboards to millions of users.

### Key Steps in Railway Website Deployment:
- **Planning & Development**: Designing responsive UI/UX, integrating APIs for live train status, PNR enquiry, seat availability, and payment gateways.
- **Server & Hosting Setup**: Deploying on secure cloud servers (AWS, Azure, or dedicated data centers) with load balancing for high traffic.
- **Security Implementation**: SSL certificates, DDoS protection, data encryption, and compliance with railway cybersecurity standards.
- **Testing**: Rigorous performance, security, and user acceptance testing under peak load conditions.
- **Go-Live & Monitoring**: Smooth rollout with continuous monitoring, auto-scaling, and regular updates.

A well-deployed railway website ensures 24/7 availability, faster booking experience, transparent operations, and improved passenger satisfaction.
